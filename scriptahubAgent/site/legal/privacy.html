2. Information processed

The AI Librarian compares a question with the catalogue on the visitor’s device. The question is stored in the URL fragment, which browsers do not send to the web server as part of the page request.

Site deliveryThe hosting provider may process IP address, request time, requested path, user agent and security logs to deliver and protect the site. Complete the provider, location and retention period: [HOSTING PROVIDER / COUNTRIES / RETENTION].
Local preferencesLanguage, light or dark theme and text size are stored in localStorage after the visitor chooses them. They stay on that device until removed in browser settings.
AI LibrarianQuestions and ranking calculations remain on the device. No question is submitted to ScriptaHub by the recommendation feature itself.
Optional dictationIf a visitor activates dictation, the browser’s speech-recognition service processes microphone input under the browser provider’s terms. ScriptaHub does not receive the audio.
Account contributions and notificationsAuthenticated forms send documents and requests directly to ScriptaHub. The account ID and verified email address come from UserPersisto; submitted documents and messages are stored privately for evaluation. EmailAgent sends status notifications to the verified account address through the configured email provider. Proposed retention: [PERIOD].
Reader analyticsThe online reader loads Umami Cloud. Umami documents that its standard tracker uses no cookies and records anonymised traffic metrics such as page views, referrer, browser, operating system, device type and country. Confirm account region, retention, contract and any custom-event configuration: [DETAILS].

3. Purposes and legal bases

Proposed purposes are delivering and securing the site, remembering requested interface preferences, answering messages, assessing contributions and understanding aggregate reader use. For visitors in the EEA, record the applicable GDPR legal basis for each purpose, normally performance of a requested service, legitimate interests, consent where required, or steps requested before a contract. For Swiss visitors, processing must follow the principles and information duties of the Federal Act on Data Protection. Complete the operator’s balancing tests and records before relying on legitimate interests.
